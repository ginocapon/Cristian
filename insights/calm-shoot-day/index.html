The Calm Shoot-Day Playbook for Happier Crews and Better Footage

A calm set is not luck. It is a call sheet that tells the truth, a first shot that is easy to win, and a crew that knows who is allowed to say “hold” without starting a debate.

We keep the first hour small: lights, faces, one usable master. Then we open the day. People work better when the first clip already exists.
