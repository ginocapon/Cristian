About studio

We're an independent video production studio based in New York City, Working with clients locally and around the world

Based in New York City, we're a creative studio specializing in video production, post-production, and motion design.

Our team brings together filmmakers, editors, motion designers, producers, and creative strategists who believe that great storytelling comes from combining different perspectives under one roof.

Every member of the studio contributes a unique skill set, allowing us to manage the entire production process—from concept development and scriptwriting to filming, editing, color grading, sound design, animation, and final delivery.

How we work

From the first rough thought to the final polished delivery, every step stays clear, collaborative, and pleasantly low-drama.
